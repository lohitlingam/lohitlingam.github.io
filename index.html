05 / Conversational AI & expansion B2C · Remitly

Conversational AI:
Taking WhatsApp to 18 markets in one year.

Helped shape the acquisition strategy and positioning for Remitly’s WhatsApp sending experience, supporting its expansion to 18 markets within one year. Connected customer insights, messaging, and channel growth to introduce a familiar way to start sending money.

Acquisition strategy Product positioning Market expansion

My role: Acquisition strategy, customer messaging, and go-to-market expansion.

Explore the WhatsApp launch and expansion

The challenge

Reach customers who were comfortable using WhatsApp but hesitant to adopt digital money transfers. Establish a compelling reason to try Remitly through conversation, then expand the channel across markets.

The customer insight

The barrier wasn’t simply access to technology—it was confidence in trying a different way to send money. A conversational experience offered customers a familiar place to ask questions, understand pricing, and get started without downloading another app.

What I owned

Contributed to the channel’s acquisition strategy, identifying how a familiar messaging experience could help customers move from consideration to their first transfer.

Helped shape positioning and customer messaging around ease, confidence, and getting started without downloading another app. Partnered with Product, regional Marketing, and channel teams to bring that proposition to market and support expansion.

The go-to-market approach

Connected paid media, landing pages, community outreach, and lifecycle messaging to introduce WhatsApp as a new entry point into Remitly. The proposition focused on customer value: explore the service, get answers, and begin a transfer through a familiar conversation.

A key positioning decision

Lead with ease and confidence in getting started. Previous marketing learnings suggested that emphasizing customer support could create anxiety about potential problems, so acquisition messaging prioritized the sending experience.

What it enabled

Supported the channel’s expansion to 18 markets within one year, creating a broader acquisition entry point and a foundation for learning how conversational experiences can support digital adoption. Early customer behavior informed improvements to the product and marketing experience.
