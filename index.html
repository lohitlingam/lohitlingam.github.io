Explore the Canada launch

FX boost Fee waiver No promotion

Explore the experimentation program

Understand when an incentive creates incremental value and when the business can reduce spending without undermining acquisition or retention.

05 / Product experience 2026

Making promotional value
clear at every step.

Co-authored the Canada serial merchandising requirements so customers could understand the current offer, remaining benefits, and eligibility across their journey.

What do I get? What remains? When does it expire?

My role: Journey requirements, product tradeoffs, and measurement planning

Explore the merchandising work

The challenge

An acquisition campaign can promise multiple benefits, but the product experience must explain which benefit applies now and which remain available.

What I owned

Co-authored the BRD covering onboarding, home-screen messaging, the calculator, send summary, and proposed benefit tracking. Defined needs for redemption status, expiry, transfer number, and currency-specific eligibility.

A key decision

Distinguished initial banner support from richer widgets and trackers. Accounted for dual-currency corridors so customers would see FX messaging only where the benefit applied.

What it enabled

A shared specification and phased delivery approach, with acquisition conversion as the primary measurement focus and repeat-transfer behavior as a supporting outcome. Richer surfaces were specified for subsequent development.
